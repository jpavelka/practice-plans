CYCLE OF POSSESSION: Work on all four functions, but do it chronologically – first work on “popping away” from the defender to create time and space, second work on a good first touch, third work on escaping the pressure (by taking a touch to the side, or turning and facing away from the defender, or doing a skill move); fourth work on accurate pass to the player with the most time and space. Each of these functions should be done so as to create the maximum amount of time and space.

Escaping the pressure: what to do depends on how close the defender is to you and how fast he is coming (how much time and space you have).

If you have no time and space immediately turn and face away, then take a touch to the side to create space
If you have a little time and space take a touch to the side to create space to pass, shoot, or dribble away
If you have even more time you can do a skill move (fake, change direction and change speed)
 

Important technical and tactical points to emphasize:

When receiving or passing with the inside of the foot make sure your toes are pointing up (so the ball does not pop up or roll over your foot)
When receiving or passing with the outside of the foot point your toe down (same reason…)
When passing, push through the ball and lift the kicking leg up – do not jab the kicking foot down on the ground.
Before receiving, turn your body and open yourself up to face the field of play, and to face your team mates, so you can pass before you are under pressure. This gives you more time and space.
After passing move to space to give your team mates passing options.
Players off the ball must provide passing options on the right side, left side and middle (split pass).
Emphasize the cycle of possession: Good first touch (one that creates time and space); escape the pressure (to create time and space to pass); accurate pass (to the player with the most time and space)
The cue to make a run is when the player with the ball takes a preparation touch and looks up. The cue to pass is when the runner starts the run. So the run comes first. Otherwise always pass to the feet. When passing to the runner pass so the ball meets the feet as well…
Attackers must always be aware what options the defenders are taking away, and what options they are giving you. Take what they give you.
Start making these coaching points with the warmup exercises, and carry them over throughout the progression.
