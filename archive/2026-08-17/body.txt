DEMONSTRATION-OBSERVATION-CORRECTION CYCLE

You must demonstrate each skill move to your players, and then have them repeat them 20 times.
Do not talk while demonstrating. Just do the skill move yourself at game speed, with no talking. Just do the move like you want them to do it in a game situation.
Have the players repeat it 20 times and observe them. Players sit down when done.
Demonstrate again – do it at speed. Then break it down into its two or three component parts. Here you can explain each part, but be brief.
Have the players repeat the skill move 20 times and sit down when done.
So the process is as follows: demonstrate, observe the players, demonstrate again to make corrections, observe the players, and repeat this process until the players’ performance improves. Not all players will master every move on the first session. It will take several sessions.
Repeat this for each skill move.
Work on five or six skill moves each practice session, and make sure the players are using those specific moves in the exercise progression for that day.
Our objective is for all players, regardless of their athletic ability, to master every one of these skill moves.

HOW TO ESCAPE THE PRESSURE FROM A DEFENDER

These points refer to when and why players must each of these skill moves in a game: to create time and space for themselves and escape the pressure from a defender.
Any time you have the ball there will be a defender right in front of you. You must dribble to the side in order to escape the pressure. There are four basic ways to do this: cut to the side, turn and face away, sole delay, and skill moves. Choose the right way depending on how close the defender is to you.
If you have lots of space between you and the defender: use skill moves like the scissors or step-overs
If you have a couple yards between you and the defender: use the twist, pull-push, pull-cut, sole delay, Maradona. But the fastest and safest way to escape pressure in this situation is a simple cut to the side.
If the defender is really close you must TURN AND FACE AWAY immediately. Use the inside cut, Cruyff, hook turn, or dragback

This week the focus is on dribbling, and specifically escaping the pressure from a defender. See the list of exercises for each day, above.
We start with basic moves to beat a defender. By the end of the season we want every player to master every one of these skill moves.
Most of these are possession games, and you must focus your coaching points on dribbling to escape the pressure from a defender, so you create enough time and space to make a safe pass or to get a shot. You should all start introducing your players to the four stages of the cycle of possession (1 - pop away from the defender to create time and space, 2- good first touch when you receive the ball, 3 - escape the pressure to create time and space to pass, 4 - accurate pass to the feet, and repeat the entire sequence).
Players must use the correct technique when receiving or dribbling: take small soft touches. Quick feet and small steps so you can react quickly. Dribble side to side, not forward into pressure. Always be aware what spaces the defenders are taking away and what spaces they are giving you. Take what they give you. Shield the ball when necessary.
Players must use the correct technique when passing (contact the ball with the inside of the heel, push through the ball and swing the kicking leg up – do not jab at the ball, or jab your kicking foot into the ground).
Players must use the correct technique when receiving the ball – small touch so you can play it immediately. Use the correct surface of the foot – inside of the heel, or sole of the foot if you are in a tight space.
