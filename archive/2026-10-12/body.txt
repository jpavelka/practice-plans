Give & Go Progression 1 – Activity 1
2V1 Pass Or Dribble
2v1 Three Zone Gauntlet
2V2+1+1… 4 Large Goals
 

Important technical points to emphasize:

When receiving or passing with the inside of the foot make sure your toes are pointing up (so the ball does not pop up or roll over your foot)
When receiving or passing with the outside of the foot point your toe down (same reason…)
When passing, push through the ball and lift the kicking leg up – do not jab the kicking foot down on the ground.
Important tactical points to emphasize:

AWARENESS: Watch the defenders – see what options they are taking away, and what options they are giving you. Take what they give you!
OPTIONS: Give-&-Go, lateral pass, through pass (forward), overlapping runs, backpass to through pass.
Dribble directly at the defender to make them commit to the ball, creating time and space for the supporting attacker.
The supporting attacker must support at a 90 degree angle (ball to defender to supporting player), and face the space for the return pass.
The initial pass must be to the feet. The return pass must be to space.
Communicate when making the initial pass: say “one-two”
After the initial pass you must sprint forward, making a blind side run.
Attackers must always be aware what options the defenders are taking away, and what options they are giving you. Take what they give you. In these exercises the basic choice is between the forward dribble or the pass. Other options might be the forward through pass and diagonal blind side run – it all depends on the position of the defenders.
The defenders can take several options away, but they can never take all the options away. There is always a solution. Attackers must think fast and be creative to find the combination plays that the defenders left open. There is always a solution. Can you find it fast enough, and execute it accurately?
Start making these coaching points with the warmup exercises, and carry them over throughout the progression.
